About

I started with the interface. I learned to redesign the system behind it.

My work sits between customer experience, technology, operations, and measurement. I am strongest when the business problem is not cleanly owned by one team, one tool, or one metric.

From digital design to product systems

I began in digital design, but the work repeatedly expanded when the visible customer experience was constrained by the systems behind it: product data, workflows, platform decisions, analytics gaps, search visibility, and unclear ownership.

That path moved me into product ownership, digital operations, commerce architecture, lifecycle marketing, analytics, and AI-enabled production systems. I am comfortable translating between business needs and implementation details because I have owned both the strategy and the execution.

The common thread across my work is structure: turning fragmented information, unclear workflows, and disconnected systems into something a team can operate, measure, and improve.

Where I am strongest

  • Complex commerce and product-data environments
  • Ambiguous problems without a single obvious owner
  • Workflows that require both automation and human judgment
  • Digital ecosystems where customer experience, operations, search, and analytics intersect
  • Translating technical findings into business decisions

Target roles

Product Manager, Product Owner, Digital Operations, UX-focused Product, Commerce Strategy, Growth Systems, and systems-oriented product leadership roles.

Tools and platforms

Hands-on: Shopify, GA4, Google Search Console, Klaviyo, HTML/CSS, Adobe Creative Cloud, and LLM-assisted workflows.

Working familiarity: Figma, Webflow, Jira, Notion, Airtable, and Quickbase.

Recent engagement systems: Prism and Lightspeed.

A consistent strength is learning unfamiliar platforms quickly enough to map dependencies, identify source-of-truth issues, and guide implementation. In my most recent consulting engagement, Prism and Lightspeed were both new to me at the outset.
