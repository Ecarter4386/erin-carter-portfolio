Diagnosing Revenue Decline in a Mobile-First Commerce Ecosystem

An analytics and strategy case study separating traffic loss, conversion behavior, technical experience, and modeled recovery opportunities.

The diagnostic challenge

Revenue was declining across multiple channels, but the signals did not point to one obvious cause. Search revenue, Safari Mobile traffic, social visibility, product discovery, and marketing capacity were under pressure while some conversion and profitability indicators were improving.

Was the business losing revenue because the digital experience was failing, or because too few qualified customers were reaching an increasingly effective storefront?

Separating symptoms from causes

I connected four evidence layers rather than treating blended revenue as a diagnosis:

01

Acquisition

Traffic sources, search visibility, feeds, referrals, and audience reach.

02

Experience

Browser, device, mobile behavior, performance, and usability.

03

Conversion

Sessions, orders, rate changes, customer type, and average order value.

04

Revenue quality

Volume, margin, retention, and the cost of recovering demand.

Recovery priorities

  • Restore product visibility through search, product feeds, collection architecture, and structured data
  • Use owned-audience quality and lifecycle systems to reduce dependence on constrained media spend
  • Maintain mobile and Safari experience quality while shifting resources toward acquisition recovery
  • Separate implemented improvements, diagnostic findings, and future recommendations in reporting
  • Prioritize low-cost structural work with measurable revenue potential
