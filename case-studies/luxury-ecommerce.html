Overview

A luxury multi-brand retailer did not have one isolated digital problem. Storefront performance, product information, merchandising, email, search visibility, analytics, imagery, and day-to-day publishing all influenced one another. Improving a single page or campaign would not create durable growth unless the operating system behind it also changed.

The work reframed “improve the website” as a product and operations challenge: create a connected digital ecosystem that the business could operate, measure, and extend.

The challenge

Customer-facing symptoms

  • Inconsistent product-detail experiences
  • Mobile performance and usability constraints
  • Weak product discoverability across search and shopping surfaces
  • Disconnected lifecycle and retention touchpoints

Structural causes

  • Unstructured product information
  • Repeated manual decisions and informal QA
  • Analytics reviewed without a shared diagnostic framework
  • Content, imagery, merchandising, and technical work operating as separate functions

What I owned

As Digital Director and Product Owner for Digital & AI Systems, I owned both strategy and execution across the ecosystem.

  • Shopify platform direction, theme requirements, PDP architecture, and live-site maintenance
  • Product taxonomy, metafield logic, structured content, image standards, and publishing QA
  • Klaviyo campaign and lifecycle strategy, audience development, and performance analysis
  • GA4, Search Console, Core Web Vitals, SEO, and behavioral diagnosis
  • Requirements translation, user stories, acceptance criteria, engineering partnership, and vendor coordination
  • Human-in-the-loop AI workflows for product descriptions, metadata, alt text, and imagery

Transformation architecture

1. Customer experience and storefront

Modernized the Shopify experience, clarified product-information hierarchy, improved mobile behavior, and prioritized technical changes against real-user performance evidence.

2. Product information and operations

Created reusable PDP structures, metafield taxonomy, controlled values, content standards, image protocols, and intake-to-publication workflows across thousands of SKUs.

3. Lifecycle and owned-audience growth

Connected campaign execution, consented audience development, segmentation, content, and conversion measurement into a stronger retention system.

4. Analytics and growth diagnosis

Connected browser, device, traffic, conversion, search, technical performance, and financial context to distinguish symptoms from causes and prioritize constrained resources.
